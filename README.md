# AskY: Tarot de Marseille for Claude and ChatGPT

AskY gives Tarot de Marseille readings in one reader's voice, in English and Spanish. This connector lets you ask for them from inside your AI chat.

- Website: https://askyreadings.com
- How to connect: https://askyreadings.com/connect
- Remote MCP server (streamable HTTP): `https://askyreadings.com/mcp`
- Official MCP Registry name: `com.askyreadings/asky`

## What it does

- **Card of the day**: the same card for everyone each day, with a short line for the day.
- **Card meanings**: the published guide to any of the 78 cards, by its French, English or Spanish name, or by number.
- **A reading on your question**: cards drawn for you and read in the reader's own words.
- **Personal readings**: information about the readings the reader writes herself for one question.

Languages: English and Spanish.

## Connect

1. In Claude, open Settings, then Connectors, add a custom connector and paste `https://askyreadings.com/mcp`.
2. Or follow the steps with pictures at https://askyreadings.com/connect.
